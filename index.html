Help me choose never puts a summer piece and a winter piece in one outfit. All-season goes with both.

Other colors in this piece (optional)

A pattern can have several colors. Help me choose checks that a solid piece goes with every color in a patterned one.

Help me choose ranks two bold patterns together lower, and treats a bold pattern with a subtle one as fine. Nothing is ruled out. Anything left as Unknown is never guessed: it will say when it couldn’t check how patterns fit.
