# The-Rack 👗📅

**The-Rack** is a lightweight, web-based digital closet and weekly outfit planning app. It helps you organize your wardrobe, schedule looks week by week, track wear frequency to avoid repeating outfits too soon, and keep track of daily outfits.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Digital Closet Drawer:** Upload photos of your tops, bottoms, shoes, and accessories directly from your device.
- **Weekly Calendar Grid:** Drag and drop your clothing items across a 7-day visual planner (Monday through Sunday) to map out your week in advance.
- **Outfit Repetition Alerts:** Automatically flags when you've worn the exact same combination of items recently to keep your rotation fresh.
- **Weather-Aware Suggestions:** Features a quick forecast banner to help guide your daily style decisions based on the weather.
- **Local Persistence:** Saves your uploaded closet and weekly schedule securely right in your browser's local storage.

---

## 🛠️ Project Structure

- `The Rack.dc.html` — The core HTML markup containing the app layout, closet sidebar, and weekly calendar grid container.
- `styles.css` — Modern, responsive styling using CSS Grid and Flexbox for clean multi-column layouts.
- `image-slot.js` — Client-side logic managing file uploads, local storage state, drag-and-drop interactions, and repetition-checking algorithms.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone or Download** this repository.
2. Open `The Rack.dc.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Click **"+ Add Closet Item"** to upload photos of your wardrobe.
4. Drag items from your closet sidebar and drop them onto any day of the week to build your outfit schedule!
