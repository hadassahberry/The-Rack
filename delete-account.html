Delete Your Account

The Rack · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open The Rack and sign in.
  2. Open the Settings tab and, under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password (or, if you signed up with Google, sign in with Google again when asked).
  4. Tap Delete my account.

Deletion happens right away.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email address and sign-in details).
  • Every clothing photo and item in your closet, with its details.
  • Your outfit calendar and your saved outfits.
  • Your settings.
  • Your saved location and cached weather and holiday information on that device.

This can't be undone. Copies held in our providers' routine backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Can't sign in?

If you can't get into your account, email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete it: therack5787@gmail.com. We'll delete your account and its data within 30 days.
