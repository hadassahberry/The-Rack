Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The Rack is a digital closet and outfit planner. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't show ads, and we don't sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile Google shares (such as your name and email address).
  • Your closet content. The photos of clothing you add, plus the names, categories, occasions, seasons and colors you give them, your outfit calendar (which items you planned for which days, and any activity labels you give an outfit, such as “Class”), and your saved outfits.
  • Your location (optional). If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to find your city, or you can type a city instead. It is used only to show the weather forecast and Jewish holiday and candle-lighting times for your area. Your chosen location is saved on your device only, not in your account. In Settings you can turn the weather and the Hebrew calendar on or off; anything switched off is never requested, so your location is not sent to that service. If both are off, the app does not use your location at all.
  • Your settings. Your Settings choices (such as whether the weather, the Hebrew calendar and outfit photos are shown) are saved with your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Camera. The camera is used only when you choose “Take a photo”. The photo you take is added to your closet like any other photo.

How your information is stored

Your account and closet content are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Each user's data is kept separately, and the database is set up so that you can read and change only your own data. Information is encrypted in transit.

Who receives information

We do not sell your information and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. To provide the app's features, some information goes to these service providers:

  • Google (Firebase) — account sign-in, database, and hosting of the app. Google may log technical information such as your IP address when you use the app.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — receives the city you type, or your approximate coordinates, to return the weather forecast. Open-Meteo states that its server logs, which may include coordinates, are deleted after 90 days.
  • Hebcal (hebcal.com) — receives your approximate coordinates and time zone to return Jewish holidays and candle-lighting times.
  • BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com) — if you let the app use your device's location, your coordinates and IP address are sent to turn them into a city name. This is done directly from your device.

We may also disclose information if required to by law.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept until you delete it. You can remove individual items at any time. In Settings you can also export a copy of everything in your closet, or clear your closet while keeping your account. To delete your whole account, open The Rack, go to Settings, and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your login and everything in your closet, outfits and planner. See how to delete your account for details, including how to ask us to delete it if you can't sign in. Copies held in our providers' routine backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Children

The Rack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
